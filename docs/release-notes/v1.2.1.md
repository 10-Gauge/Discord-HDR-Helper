Discord HDR Helper 1.2.1 fixes game audio not reaching your Discord viewers.

Thanks to [@AndrixPro100GOD](https://github.com/AndrixPro100GOD) for finding the cause and contributing the fix.

## What's fixed

- **Viewers hear your audio again.** The old "Automatic" mode replayed audio at zero volume, but Windows hands Discord the audio *after* that volume is applied, so viewers got silence. The replay now always plays at full volume and unmuted.
- **Clearer audio errors.** If capturing an app fails, the Audio tab shows which step failed and the Windows error code, and the helper retries every few seconds instead of giving up.
- **Less CPU use.** Changing audio settings could make the audio thread spin; it doesn't anymore.

## Heads up

- Because the replay is now audible, you'll hear your audio twice if it plays on the speakers or headset you use. In the **Audio** tab, pick an output you don't listen to, such as your monitor's speakers.
- Keep the helper unmuted in the Windows Volume Mixer, or viewers won't hear anything.
- Audio passthrough needs Windows 11 (or Windows build 20348 or newer).

## Download

Download **DiscordHDRHelper.exe** below. There's nothing to install. If SmartScreen warns you, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
