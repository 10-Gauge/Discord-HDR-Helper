Discord HDR Helper 1.1.3 sends your stream at full native resolution.

## Fixes

- **No more jagged lines in the stream.** The output window was sized to fit inside the taskbar-free part of your screen, so a full-screen 4K source got shrunk by a few percent. That slight shrink turns fine detail and edges into stair-steps and shimmering lines. The output now always holds the picture pixel for pixel at the source's resolution, whatever size the window is.
- The output window can now be exactly your monitor's size, so a full-screen source fits 1:1. It stays behind your other windows as before.

## Download

Download **DiscordHDRHelper.exe** below. There's nothing to install. If SmartScreen warns you, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

For the sharpest stream, set Discord's stream resolution to **Source**.
