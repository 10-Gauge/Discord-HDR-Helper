Discord HDR Helper 1.1.4 makes audio capture sturdier and says why audio isn't flowing when it can't capture it.

## Fixes

- **Audio capture reads data more reliably.** The helper now reads captured audio until the buffer is empty, instead of relying on a size check that some Windows builds report as zero.
- **Clearer audio status.** The Audio tab now notes, next to each app, when Windows gives the helper no audio data, gives it only silence, or refuses to capture the app. That shows whether the problem is the app (some anti-cheat protected games block capture) or the setup.

## Download

Download **DiscordHDRHelper.exe** below. There's nothing to install. If SmartScreen warns you, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
