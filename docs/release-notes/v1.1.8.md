Discord HDR Helper 1.1.8 puts one control front and center: **Stream brightness**.

## What's new

- **Stream brightness slider.** It's the first thing on the *Picture* tab, and it sets how bright the stream looks to viewers. It starts on *Automatic*, which uses your Windows *SDR content brightness* setting. If the stream is too dark or washed out, drag it until it looks right; it updates live.
- The tonemapping controls (brightness, highlight peak, curve) are now tucked under **Advanced tonemapping**, since most people never need them.

## Download

Download **DiscordHDRHelper.exe** below. There's nothing to install. If SmartScreen warns you, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
