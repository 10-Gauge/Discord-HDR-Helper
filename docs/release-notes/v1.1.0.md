Discord HDR Helper 1.1 adds **audio passthrough**, so your game sound now goes out with the stream.

## What's new

- **Game audio in your stream.** When you share a window, Discord sends sound only from the app that owns that window. The helper now records your game's audio (or every app's audio except Discord when you capture a whole display) and replays it from its own window, so Discord picks it up. No virtual audio cable or driver is needed.
- **Silent by default.** The replay plays on your speakers at zero volume, so you don't hear anything twice. If viewers get no sound on your setup, choose an output device you don't listen to in the new **Audio** tab.
- **New Audio tab** with an on/off switch, a choice between the captured app and all apps, the output device, and a live level meter that shows which apps are being sent.
- The settings panel is now split into **Picture**, **Audio** and **Options** tabs.

## Download

Download **DiscordHDRHelper.exe** below. There's nothing to install. If SmartScreen warns you, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Make sure **Sound** is turned on in Discord's stream settings.
